# Focus

Preserve a visible focus indication and a logical focus order. Check that focused controls are not entirely hidden by sticky content or overlays. Do not remove outlines without a verified replacement matching the approved design.

For future modal interfaces, define initial focus, containment while open, Escape behavior where appropriate, and focus restoration on close. Do not apply focus trapping to ordinary nonmodal regions.

Check keyboard focus in each theme, state, scroll position, and responsive layout. Record browser and assistive-technology evidence in the runtime checklist. Figma focus examples do not demonstrate working browser focus management.
